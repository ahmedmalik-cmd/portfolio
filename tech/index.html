01 / Senior Thesis

real & rendered

Real-time CV · Local LLM · 2026

Live 3D point clouds of the people in the room, read in real time by a local vision-language model. The installation watches its visitors and tries, out loud, to say what it sees.

What I built

Depth cameras feed a single merged point cloud, reconstructed and rendered live in Unity. A locally-run vision-language model (LLaVA via Ollama) looks at the scene frame by frame and generates a running "perception" — language the space speaks back as it watches. Nothing leaves the machine; the whole perception loop runs on local hardware in the gallery.

02 / Live Installation

Projection Mapping — Jadu at AWE

Unity · Panasonic PT-MZ20K · On-site AV

A large-format projection mapped onto the venue for a Jadu mixer at Augmented World Expo — rigged, calibrated, run, and struck on site.

What I ran

Volunteering with Jadu, an AR company, I ran the projection mapping for their AWE mixer end to end. The projector was a Panasonic PT-MZ20K — a 20,000-lumen WUXGA 3LCD laser unit, liquid-cooled and a two-person lift. I drove the mapped content from Unity and aligned it to the venue on site, output at the projector's native 1920×1200 with everything off the target masked to true black (at 20,000 lumens, spill is real). Then I struck and packed the rig: the tool-free lens removal — top slide cover, lock lever, counter-clockwise out — lens cased on its own, body cased and left to acclimate to room temperature before power-up to avoid condensation.

03 / Film · Process

Physical → Generated

Handmade sets · Generative video

Handmade sets, slides, and footage used as the seed for generative video — the built and the synthesized cut into one another. The work is in the process, not only the final film.

Process

My films start in physical material — handmade sets, slides, and footage shot by hand — which I then hand to generative video to re-animate and extend, cutting the built and the synthesized back into each other. Return Without Reason began as an analog experiment after Stan Brakhage, until AI reanimates it; Loop Prison carries the method into an episodic series.
