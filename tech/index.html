01 / Senior Thesis

real & rendered

Real-time CV · Local LLM · 2026

Live 3D point clouds of the people in the room, read in real time by a local vision-language model. The installation watches its visitors and tries, out loud, to say what it sees.

What I built

Depth cameras feed a single merged point cloud, reconstructed and rendered live in Unity. A locally-run vision-language model (LLaVA via Ollama) looks at the scene frame by frame and generates a running "perception" — language the space speaks back as it watches. Nothing leaves the machine; the whole perception loop runs on local hardware in the gallery.

Stack

  • Unity
  • Depth sensing
  • Computer vision
  • Python
  • Ollama
  • LLaVA
  • Projection

02 / Live Installation

Projection Mapping — Jadu at AWE

Unity · Panasonic PT-MZ20K · On-site

A large-format projection mapped onto the venue for a Jadu mixer at Augmented World Expo — set up, aligned, run, and struck on site.

What I built

Volunteering with Jadu, an AR company, I handled the projection mapping for their mixer at AWE end to end: rigging and configuring a Panasonic PT-MZ20K (a 20,000-lumen laser projector), driving the mapped content from Unity, aligning the output to the venue's surfaces on site, operating it live through the event, and tearing the whole rig down afterward.

Stack

  • Unity
  • Panasonic PT-MZ20K
  • Projection mapping
  • On-site calibration
